HOW TO PLAY

The left card shows a player and their England caps. Guess whether the player on the right won more or fewer.

Get it right and he becomes your new card. Level on caps counts as a pass. One wrong call and you're out.

Every player appears once per game. Clear the whole deck to complete the squad. Caps for players still in the England setup are refreshed from Wikipedia twice a week.
